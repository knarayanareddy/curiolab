Together at the table

  1. Place the LabMat on a flat, well-lit surface.
  2. Put 12 coins beside—not on—the mat.
  3. Build a bridge with crates, then add coins one at a time.

Materials: printed LabMat, 12 coins, 4 small crates or blocks.

State contract

Observation ≠ result

  • Aligned
    All four markers named, not just green corners.
  • Needs review
    The current count can be stopped, corrected, or replaced by TV entry.
  • Camera denied
    Preview disappears; manual route moves to the top.
  • Reconnecting
    Last observed value keeps its timestamp and is not called confirmed.
Interaction and state annotations
  • Primary action changes with observation state.
  • Permission denial and occlusion never block remote-only play.
  • Count confirmation remains a family decision on either surface.
