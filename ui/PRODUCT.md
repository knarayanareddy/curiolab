# CurioLab — Product Truth

## Audience

One or two children ages 7–11 with an adult, sharing a Fire TV. A companion Android phone is a lab instrument during setup and measurement, not the center of attention.

## Core job

**Make a prediction, test it with real materials, and see the shared story respond to the confirmed result.**

## Operating context

- Ten-foot Fire TV story experience with remote focus and captions.
- Low-table household experiment, variable lighting, adult-led camera setup, and possible network failure.
- Children with different reading, motor, sensory, and speech needs; no child accounts or public sharing.

## Product personality

Curious, warm, cinematic, and precise. A storybook laboratory with a moon-base setting—not a classroom dashboard, cartoon baby app, or glowing AI toy.

## Voice

- `What do you predict?`
- `Your flat bridge held 4 coins.`
- `The folded bridge held 7. What changed between the two tests?`
- `The camera needs another look. You can also enter the count on the TV.`

Avoid: `You're wrong!`, `AI thinks you're smart`, exaggerated cheering, or claiming one trial proves a universal law.

## Durable constraints

- The physical experiment and measured result remain the visual climax.
- Story decoration never obscures instructions, focus, captions, or counts.
- Every camera interaction has a visible remote alternative.
- Companion UI is utilitarian and adult-led; TV UI carries the narrative.
- Small-object supervision and no unsafe action prompts are part of the layout, not fine print.

## Visual anti-references

- Generic purple edtech dashboards and lesson grids.
- Claymorphic buttons, inflated cards, and excessive bouncing that compete with the experiment.
- Robot mascots, emoji icons, child-performance leaderboards, and streaks.
- Dark scenes with low-contrast captions or neon glow as the only focus state.

## Design success signals

- A child can find the prediction and confirmation controls from across the room.
- An adult can understand calibration failure and switch to manual mode without stopping the episode.
- Exact counts remain readable while the scene animates.
- The result feels magical because the story responds, not because the interface is visually noisy.
